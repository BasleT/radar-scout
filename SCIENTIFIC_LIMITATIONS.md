# SCIENTIFIC_LIMITATIONS.md (permanent; update with every phase)

Last updated: 2026-11-02 (Phases 1-4). Status key: **OPEN** = unresolved; **KNOWN** = inherent in the data or model, only documentable; **MITIGATED** = handled in design, to be verified.

| ID | Limitation | Status | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Reflectivity (dBZ) measures backscattered power from hydrometeors within a pulse volume. It is not a cloud boundary, liquid water content, rain rate, or hazard level. Absence of echo does not mean absence of cloud. | KNOWN | UI wording avoids "cloud". Thresholds are labelled "reflectivity ≥ X dBZ". |
| L2 | Radar beam is about 1° wide. Sample volumes grow with range (about 1.7 km wide at 100 km, using 0.96°, own arithmetic to be verified). Vertical position of a gate is the beam-center height, not an exact location. | KNOWN | Per-sample beamwidth and half-width stored. Gates drawn as volumes, not points. |
| L3 | Beam height assumes the standard 4/3 effective Earth radius. Real refraction varies, and ducting or super-refraction can displace beams by amounts not recoverable without sounding data. | KNOWN | Stated on every height. Sensitivity bound via ray-trace (VALIDATION_PLAN R5). |
| L4 | Coverage gaps: cone of silence above the top elevation cut, gaps between cuts, and beam overshoot at long range. Terrain blockage and clutter are not corrected. | KNOWN | `NOT_SCANNED` regions shown. No filling. |
| L5 | Volume scans take several minutes (VCP 12 about 4.3 min, VCP 212 about 4.6 min, from secondary sources). Sweeps are not simultaneous, and fast-evolving storms change during a volume. | KNOWN | Per-radial times preserved. Viewer shows the time spread. |
| L6 | N=0 (below threshold) and N=1 (range folded) are not reflectivity values. Range-folded gates may hide real echoes. | MITIGATED | Distinct data classes. Never rendered as a dBZ number. |
| L7 | MSL vs ellipsoid: the ICD gives site height above MSL, but the geoid model is not identified in the sections read. Cesium needs ellipsoid heights. Geoid undulation in CONUS is tens of metres. | OPEN | Both heights stored. Geoid model and version recorded. Test T12. |
| L8 | Horizontal datum of the radar position (WGS84 vs NAD83) is unstated in the sections read. Difference is about a metre in CONUS, small against 250 m gates. | OPEN | Treated as WGS84. Recorded as an assumption. |
| L9 | Terrain-relative (AGL) altitude needs a DEM with its own datum and error. | OPEN | AGL is a derived, DEM-attributed quantity only, off by default. |
| L10 | Horizontal placement: the 4/3 model gives an arc length on an effective sphere, which this engine then lays off as a WGS84 geodesic distance. Py-ART instead uses a spherical azimuthal-equidistant inverse (R=6370997 m). Measured on both cases: the two methods differ by up to 0.25% of ground arc, i.e. 1.04 km (KTLX) and 1.10 km (KDIX) at 440-459 km. Neither is a measurement of where the beam is; the true uncertainty is at least this large at long range. | KNOWN, quantified | Engine choice documented. Difference reported in every validation report (T4). Not validated against any independent truth. |
| L11 | Interpolation or smoothing between gates creates values that were not measured. Averaging in dBZ rather than linear Z is wrong in principle. Iso-surfaces imply boundaries that do not exist. | MITIGATED | Off by default. Separate artifact with `INTERPOLATED` class, domain/method recorded, and a persistent UI label. |
| L12 | Dual-polarization data quality (attenuation, calibration, non-meteorological echoes such as birds and insects) is not assessed. Only reflectivity is in scope. | KNOWN | No QC beyond the source's own flags. |
| L13 | Doviak & Zrnić Eq. 2.28 has been verified only through secondary sources (Py-ART, wradlib docs), not the book. | OPEN | See VALIDATION_PLAN §8 item 2. |
| L14 | Beamwidth values (0.88° to 0.96°) come from non-authoritative search summaries. | OPEN | Confirm in NEXRAD Technical Information. |
| L15 | Py-ART's high-level reader (`read_nexrad_archive`) masks N=0 and N=1 together, fills absent gates with 1 (indistinguishable from range-folded), and may interpolate mixed-resolution rays. | MITIGATED | Engine uses `NEXRADLevel2File.radial_records` directly, keeping raw N per recorded gate. Verified gate-for-gate equal to an independent from-spec reader on both cases (T1, 15.6 M gates). |
| L16 | Third-party reference tools (WCT, GridRad, MRMS) use their own gridding and Earth models, so disagreement is not automatically an error in either. | KNOWN | Classified in §6 of the validation plan. |
| L17 | Validation rests on two volumes (KTLX 2013-05-20 19:51:11 UTC, KDIX 2014-04-30 12:00:23 UTC), both from the Unidata mirror of the archive, KTLX is VCP 12 (17 elevation numbers, dual-pol), KDIX is VCP 21 (11 elevation numbers); both contain 0.5 deg and 1.0 deg azimuth-resolution radials; REF scale 2/offset 66 only. Newer VCPs, super-resolution variants, other scale/offset values, Message Type 1 files, and LDM-bzip2 container files are untested by real data. | OPEN | More cases needed before broader claims. |
| L18 | Reference-tool trap: xradar `DBZH` encodes N=0 and N=1 as -33.0 and -32.5 dBZ (not NaN): 7.76 M + 31.8 k gates in KTLX, 3.89 M + 212 k in KDIX. Using it naively shows below-threshold/range-folded gates as real (very low) reflectivity. | KNOWN | Cross-check compares only N>=2 gates; engine keeps N=0/1 as separate classes. |
| L19 | Actual elevation angle varies per radial within a nominal cut (e.g. KTLX nominal 0.5 deg cut spans 0.46-0.66 deg). Heights computed from nominal cut angles would be wrong by tens of metres at long range. | MITIGATED | Per-radial elevation is used everywhere (verified equal to spec reader). |
| L20 | Antenna altitude = site height (MSL) + tower height (AGL). The ICD calls the second field tower height; Py-ART calls it feedhorn_height and xradar reports altitude 69 m for KDIX (45 + 24). Consistent across tools, not stated by the ICD as the feedhorn altitude. | OPEN | Recorded in case.json and manifest as an assumption. |
| L21 | Test files from this era are gzip-wrapped raw message streams, not LDM-bzip2 records; the spec reader handles both but only the raw form is exercised by real data. | KNOWN | See L17. |
| L22 | wradlib's `bin_altitude` uses R+a (antenna inside the sphere) vs the Doviak-form used here (z + a): heights differ by 0.57 m (KTLX) and 0.10 m (KDIX) at most. | KNOWN, quantified | Reported as a data-processing difference, not an error. |
| L23 | Geoid: EGM2008 undulation at the radar sites is -27.5 m (KTLX) and -33.6 m (KDIX); a second published model (EGM96) agrees to 0.03 m and 0.13 m. This is a sanity check only; no independent calculator or benchmark has been used, and the geoid behind the ICD's mean-sea-level site height is unidentified. | OPEN | See L7. |
| L24 | Beam width is not decoded or applied; `beamwidth_deg` is NaN in every sample. Sample-volume geometry for rendering is therefore not yet available. | OPEN | Phase 5 prerequisite (see L14). |

| L25 | Radial velocity (VEL) is shown as stored: no dealiasing (Nyquist-folded velocities appear as wrong-sign values), no storm-motion subtraction, and the sign convention (toward/away from the radar) is not stated anywhere in the ICD text I searched (no match for toward, away, inbound, outbound), so the UI colours by sign only. Range-folded gates (N=1) are not velocities. | OPEN | Find the convention in a primary source (e.g. the RPG/WSR-88D product documentation); legend states sign only. |
| L26 | SW, ZDR, PHI, RHO are decoded and unit-converted (raw N matches the independent reader gate-for-gate) but have no quality control: no attenuation or calibration correction, no ZDR bias removal, PHI is the raw differential phase (not KDP, not unfolded), RHO is not used for clutter/biological filtering. Their unit labels come from a garbled extraction of Table XVII-I. | KNOWN | Shown as measured values only; no derived dual-pol products (hail, rain rate, hydrometeor class) are computed. |
| L27 | Only REF has its full per-gate table persisted (`samples.parquet`). Other products are reduced straight to the viewer artifact; each gate is still traceable by source SHA-256 + radial_index + gate_idx, but there is no stored full table. | KNOWN | Re-run the engine to regenerate. |
| L28 | The viewer's display thresholds (e.g. VEL ≥ 5 m/s, RHO ≥ 0.95) are display choices only and carry no scientific meaning. The viewer draws points, not sample volumes, because beam width is not decoded (L24). | KNOWN | Stated in the app banner. |
| L29 | The app was run in an Android emulator (API 34, x86_64) and on one Pixel phone; the server runs in Docker on pve1 against the live archive and 2013 case volumes (see VALIDATION_STATUS). WebView2 on Windows is still unchecked, and no rendering or position check has been done against an independent product. | PARTLY CLOSED | Remaining: WebView2, independent position check. |

| L30 | The viewer's "Volume" render (first version) bins gates into 1.5 km x 0.75 km voxels in the viewer and draws soft sprites coloured by the maximum measured gate in each voxel. It is a display reduction done in the wrong place (the viewer) and is not a resampled grid. | OPEN | Replaced by engine-built grid (PLAN_V2 R5); until then the on-screen label says "display reduction". |
| L31 | Planned resampled 3D volume and iso-surface (PLAN_V2 R5/R6) will be interpolated/derived data, with smooth surfaces that imply certainty the data do not carry. | PLANNED | Labelled DERIVED/INTERPOLATED in file, UI and every view; Points mode stays default. |
| L32 | Level III products (EET, DVL, N0K, HHC, NST) are computed by the NWS algorithms, not by this project. Their bucket listing was checked; file format, date coverage and decoder support were not. | UNVERIFIED | PLAN_V2 R10: shown with NWS product names, verified against an independent reader before use. |
| L33 | Splitting a volume into one polar texture per elevation cut assumes each cut has a single low-tilt entry; SAILS/MESO-SAILS repeat low cuts within one volume and are not handled specially in the engine or viewer. | OPEN | PLAN_V2 R8. |
| L34 | Velocity dealiasing and storm-relative velocity are not implemented; any storm-motion value must be an explicit labelled input, never an inferred default. | OPEN | PLAN_V2 R7 (after resolving L25). |
| L35 | Basemap tiles (Esri World Dark Gray) are cosmetic third-party raster imagery fetched over the internet; they are not data from this project, carry no accuracy claim, and positions of radar data do not depend on them. | KNOWN | Attribution shown in Cesium credits. |
| L36 | The previous default archive bucket `noaa-nexrad-level2` now returns 403 for listing and downloads; `unidata-nexrad-level2` is used instead. Bucket names and access have changed before and may change again. | KNOWN | Configurable via BUCKET_URL; case windows and event times are approximate (engine/cases.py). |

Implementation vs validation status: see `docs/VALIDATION_STATUS.md`.
